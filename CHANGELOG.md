### Safety

NuclearShield is a defensive educational demonstrator.

All included demonstration evidence is fictional and synthetic.

NuclearShield has no plant-control capability and must not be connected to operational nuclear facilities, SCADA or I&C networks, safety systems, physical-access systems, nuclear material-accounting systems, or production industrial environments.

Human authority remains the final decision boundary.
