# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

NuclearShield is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and technically focused community.

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively
- Provide useful technical feedback
- Respect differing experience levels and perspectives
- Keep discussions relevant to the project
- Protect sensitive and confidential information
- Follow the project's defensive cybersecurity and safety boundaries

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Deliberately disruptive or abusive communication
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Sharing credentials or confidential operational information
- Submitting real nuclear facility or sensitive industrial-control information
- Encouraging misuse of NuclearShield for offensive or operational interference

## Project Safety Boundary

NuclearShield is a defensive, read-only cybersecurity assurance project.

Community contributions and discussions must not introduce or request:

- Plant-control functionality
- Offensive exploitation capability
- Real nuclear facility information
- Operational SCADA or I&C credentials
- Sensitive facility topology
- Real safety-system information
- Nuclear material-accounting information
- Sensitive physical-access or personnel information

Demonstrations should use fictional or synthetic evidence.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions, issues, comments, or other participation that violates these standards or the project's safety boundaries.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from participating in the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation within the NuclearShield project and its official community spaces.

## Reporting

For ordinary project concerns, contact the repository maintainers through the appropriate GitHub channels.

For security vulnerabilities, follow the instructions in SECURITY.md and do not disclose sensitive vulnerability information in a public issue.
